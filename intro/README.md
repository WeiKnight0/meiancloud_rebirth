## Positioning
Meian Cloud is a cultural and educational website centered on Meian at Southeast University. It aims to turn scattered historical material, exhibition content, and visit-related information into an accessible online experience.

## Background
Meian is not only an important campus site, but also a place with deep historical memory and red cultural significance. Existing information is often fragmented across different pages, articles, and social media channels, which makes it harder for students, visitors, and researchers to form a clear understanding of its story.

The website was created to address that gap through a unified digital platform.

## Purpose
The website is designed to:
- introduce the history and cultural meaning of Meian
- support public understanding of the site and its exhibitions
- provide an online channel for discussion and feedback
- improve access to organized and readable reference information
- connect historical education with digital communication

## Core Features
### Historical and cultural presentation
The website introduces Meian's origins, historical figures, exhibition spaces, and broader background through structured pages and images.

![Home Page](../figs/home-page.png)

### Guided exhibition browsing
Visitors can explore exhibition-related content through the "Tracing Meian" section, which helps users move from overview information to more specific displays.

![Tracing Meian](../figs/tracing-meian.png)

### Public interaction
Registered users can join the discussion area, post comments, ask questions, and respond to others. This gives the project a feedback loop instead of making it a static display-only site.

![Free Discussion](../figs/free-discussion.png)

### FAQ and support information
The site includes a frequently asked questions section to help visitors quickly understand recurring topics, practical concerns, and historical context.

![Frequently Asked Questions](../figs/faq.png)

### User accounts
Users can register, log in, manage personal information, and participate more fully in the interactive sections of the site.

### AI-assisted inquiry
The homepage includes an AI assistant interface that can respond to user questions and improve accessibility to site content.

### About page and project context
The site also includes an "About Us" page that explains the team background, project motivation, and supporting contact channels.

![About Us](../figs/about-us.png)

## Target Users
- students and teachers at Southeast University
- visitors interested in Meian and campus history
- youth education and social practice teams
- readers interested in red culture, modern Chinese history, and digital heritage presentation

## Cultural and Educational Value
The value of Meian Cloud is not limited to introducing a building or a campus landmark. It also helps connect people with the historical context, revolutionary memory, and educational meaning associated with Meian.

By organizing content online, the project helps transform historical knowledge into something easier to learn, revisit, and share.

## Value of Digital Preservation
This website demonstrates how cultural memory can be preserved and communicated through digital tools:
- reducing information fragmentation
- improving public access to historical material
- making educational content easier to browse
- creating a bridge between offline heritage and online communication

## How to Use the Website
Typical usage flow:
1. Visit the homepage to understand the overall theme of Meian.
2. Browse the exhibition and history sections for detailed content.
3. Read the FAQ for common questions.
4. Register or log in if you want to join discussions.
5. Use the AI assistant for quick questions.

## Future Directions
Possible future expansion includes:
- richer exhibition content and archival organization
- better multilingual support
- more complete visitor guidance information
- stronger moderation and community operations
- deeper AI-assisted interpretation of historical content
